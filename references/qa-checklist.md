# QA checklist

## Research

- All supplied modalities were inspected with an appropriate extraction method.
- The selected topic is a deliberate editorial angle, not an automatic full-input summary.
- The recommended angle has clear beginner value, story potential, visual potential, and supportable scope.
- The intended meaning is explicit and the default audience is AI-curious beginners unless overridden.
- At least two credible sources support the central definition and mechanism.
- Claims, analogies, and inferences are distinguishable.
- Dates, statistics, names, and causal statements are checked.
- Important limits or exceptions are not removed for drama.

## Narrative and copy

- The set answers what, why, and how/use.
- Every card has one clear job and connects to the next.
- The opening is interesting without clickbait or false certainty.
- The definition is concise, accurate, and understandable without prior expertise.
- The recommended title is strongest; two genuine alternatives are included.
- The body is no more than 200 Chinese characters including hashtags.

## Visual consistency

- Every image is an exact 3:4 portrait ratio.
- The cover title is centered, dominant, and readable as a thumbnail.
- Font families, weights, hierarchy, margins, rules, textures, and corner furniture form one system.
- `2026`, `RiXi`, `AI`, and the topic-derived `{{关键词}}` appear consistently; the replacement contains no more than four displayed characters.
- Visual variations serve the individual card's content.
- The series follows the currently approved visual profile; older profile defaults were not carried forward after the user changed them.
- Every flow, node, particle field, label, or decorative element has a content relationship or has been removed.
- A layout-only revision preserves the approved visual style and untouched content.
- The current series keeps one coherent character design, clothing logic, linework, material, color system, typography hierarchy, and layout language.
- The composition avoids mechanical four-quadrant grids, unnecessary symmetry, evenly filled space, and repeated corner placement.
- Character placement, action, and props support the current card's narrative function and do not obstruct primary information.

## Legibility and rendering

- All Chinese characters match the approved script exactly.
- When OCR is available, OCR every final card and compare the result with the approved copy before manual inspection. Treat OCR as a warning system, not proof of correctness.
- Manually inspect every headline, concept term, corner label, page number, punctuation mark, and small caption after OCR or when OCR is unavailable.
- No mojibake, invented glyphs, clipping, overflow, accidental line breaks, or punctuation errors exist.
- Large subject glyphs retain standard structure and complete strokes; grain, blur, particles, and lines do not damage recognition.
- Explanatory text remains the visual subject and does not overlap busy imagery.
- Contrast remains strong at phone size.
- Small labels are still readable and no essential content falls outside safe margins.
- The reading path has a clear origin, sequence, convergence, and destination where the composition encodes a process.

## Version control

- Exactly one formal image is delivered per card unless the user explicitly requested comparison drafts, an A/B test, or alternatives.
- When the user asks to fix a problem in the current version, the current version is revised instead of adding another candidate.

## Snowy owl character — only when selected

- Does the snowy owl have exactly two clear black stick-figure arms?
- Were wings mistakenly used as hands?
- When clothed, are any oval wings or feather patches still visible?
- Does a scarf incorrectly emerge from inside the clothing?
- When standing, does the character have two short legs and clearly grounded claws?
- Does the overall standing silhouette resemble a bowling pin or capsule instead of a supported body?
- Is the character mechanically fixed in the lower-left corner?
- Do the character identity, action, and props genuinely serve the current card?
- Was more than one formal candidate image incorrectly delivered for a card?
- Are character appearance, clothing logic, and rendering quality consistent across the series?

## Final package

- Image count matches the approved plan and includes a cover.
- Files sort in reading order with zero-padded names.
- Title, body, image set, and source list are all delivered.
- A final visual inspection has been performed on every exported file.
